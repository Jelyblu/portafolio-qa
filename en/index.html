Baby Fresh: Sueños de Colores

Live physical-digital installation.

I built and tested Sueños de Colores, a physical-digital installation for Baby Fresh: children color a teddy bear on paper, put it into an oven-shaped installation, and watch their own drawing come to life in a shared virtual world on a big screen.

Testing focused on what a live installation does not forgive: latency, so each child connected what they drew with what they saw on screen, and tolerance for real drawings made outside the lines, without the system failing in front of an audience.
